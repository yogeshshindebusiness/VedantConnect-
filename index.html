Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2025

Vedant Connect (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users, clients, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you interact with our website, software, and services.

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us, request information, register for our services, or use our software solutions.

  1. Name

This information helps us maintain, improve, and secure our services.

Vedant Connect may use cookies and similar technologies to ensure the proper functioning of our website and software services.

Cookies may be used to:

  1. Maintain user sessions
  2. Enable secure login functionality
  3. Improve website navigation
  4. Understand general usage patterns

Users may control or disable cookies through their browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our services.

3. Platform and Service Data

Vedant Connect provides digital software solutions designed to support organizations and office operations.

Information entered into our platform may include data necessary for managing applications, communications, contacts, documents, tasks, and other operational activities.

We process such information solely for providing and improving the services requested by our clients.

4. Information Use

We may use collected information to:

  1. Provide and maintain our services

  3. Improve platform functionality and performance

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

  1. When required by applicable law or legal authorities

These third-party providers may process information only as required to provide their respective services.

  1. Encrypted data transmission where applicable

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  1. Request access to your personal information

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided on our official website.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Vedant Connect through our official communication channels.
